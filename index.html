📖 HOW TO PLAY

Classic Ludo

  • Roll a 6 to unlock tokens from the yard.
  • Move tokens around the board in clockwise order.
  • Landing on an opponent's token sends it back to their yard & grants you an extra roll!
  • Tokens on ⭐ safe cells cannot be cut.
  • You need an exact count to reach the 👑 center crown.
  • First player to get ALL 4 tokens to the crown wins!

Snakes & Ladders

  • Roll dice and move your single token forward.
  • 🪜 Ladders: Climb up & earn +25 coins and an extra turn!
  • 🐍 Snakes: Slide down to the snake's tail.
  • Need exact count to reach tile 100. Overshoot = bounce back.
  • No cutting — multiple players can share tiles.

Rage Meter

  • Fills +3% each turn. At 100%, use STAMPEDE!
  • STAMPEDE moves your token +12 tiles (ignores snakes).
