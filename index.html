📖 GAME RULES

Classic Ludo

  • Roll a 6 to unlock a token from your base yard.
  • Token Spawn: Unlocks onto your own colored starting arrow tile outside your base!
  • Dynamic Dice: Moves right next to the active player's corner profile.
  • Landing on an opponent's token sends it home and grants an extra roll!
  • ⭐ Star cells are safe zones (no captures).
  • 5-tile highlighted home path leads into the 👑 center crown. First to crown all 4 tokens wins!

Snakes & Ladders

  • Race to tile 100! Numbers are clear and readable.
  • 🪜 Ladders grant +25 coins and an extra roll!
  • 🐍 Cartoon snakes slide you down to their tail.
  • Multiple players can share the same tile safely.
