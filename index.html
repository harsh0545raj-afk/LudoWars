📖 GAME RULES

Classic Ludo

  • Roll a 6 to unlock a token from the yard.
  • Token Spawn: Unlocked tokens spawn on your own base's start tile!
  • Dynamic Dice: The dice moves right to the active player's base and glows in their color.
  • Landing on an opponent's token sends it home and grants an extra roll!
  • ⭐ Star cells are safe zones (no cutting).
  • Need an exact count to reach the center crown (👑). First with all 4 tokens home wins!

Snakes & Ladders

  • Single token per player. Race to tile 100!
  • 🪜 Ladders grant +25 coins and an extra roll!
  • 🐍 Cartoon snakes slide you down to their tail.
  • Multiple players can share the same tile safely.
  • Tile 100 requires exact roll; overshoot bounces back!

⚡ Rage & Ad Power-ups

  • Rage fills +3%/turn. At 100%, activate STAMPEDE (+12 tiles)!
  • Loaded Dice: Watch an ad to guarantee a 6 on your next roll!
  • Rage Boost: Watch an ad to instantly max your Rage to 100%!
  • Premium Dice: Watch an ad for a holographic animated neon dice!
